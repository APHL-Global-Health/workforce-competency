# Survey

The Survey page is where staff complete competency self-assessments.

## Starting a new assessment

1. Pick a **Domain** from the dropdown in the top-left (e.g. *Bioinformatics*, *Administrative Controls*).
2. Click **Start** on the welcome page.
3. For each subcompetency, pick the level that best describes your current ability:
   - **Beginner** — needs guidance to perform the task
   - **Competent** — can perform the task unsupervised
   - **Proficient** — can teach others and solve edge cases
   - **Expert** — authoritative; the person others consult
   - **N/A** — the task is outside your role

## Saving and resuming

- The survey **auto-saves** to your browser every 2 seconds. If you close the tab mid-assessment, your answers are kept.
- The hamburger (Save) icon in the top-right saves to the server too.
- If there's an unfinished session for the domain you pick, you'll see an amber **Unfinished assessment** banner with **Start over** and **Resume** buttons. Resume picks up exactly where you left off.

## Submitting

Click **Submit** on the last page. After submission:

- The assessment enters `pending` review status (admin approval required before it counts in reports).
- The **My assessments** page adds a completed card with your average score.
- You can start the same domain again to record a newer snapshot.

## Theme

The survey widget follows the app theme — toggle light/dark with the sun/moon icon, and the survey re-themes live without losing your answers.
